# Dusk privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

Dusk does not collect, store, send or sell any personal data.

- **No data leaves your browser.** Dusk makes no network requests, has no account, no
  analytics and no tracking.
- **What it saves:** your settings (the global dark mode switch, your per-site overrides and
  the Google Docs text option) in Chrome's own storage, synced with your Chrome profile if
  you have sync on. It also keeps a small local list of sites it found to be already dark,
  so they don't flash on your next visit. You can clear all of it by removing the extension.
- **What it reads on pages:** only the computed background colors, to tell whether a page is
  already dark. It does not read page text, form data or your browsing history.
- **Google Docs, Sheets and Slides:** a small script adjusts how the document canvas is
  drawn so pictures keep their colors. It does not read your documents.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
