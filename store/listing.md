# Chrome Web Store listing (draft)

**Name:** Dusk: Dark Mode

**Summary (132 chars max):**
Dark mode for every site with per-site overrides. Also darkens Google Docs, Sheets and Slides, including the document itself.

**Category:** Accessibility (alternative: Productivity)

**Language:** English

## Description

Dusk turns the web dark, and lets you decide site by site.

- One switch for dark mode everywhere.
- "Override this site" gives any site its own setting: Off, Dark or Invert.
- Pages that are already dark are detected and left alone.
- Photos and videos keep their real colors.
- No white flash while pages load.
- Works on Google Docs, Sheets and Slides: the document itself goes dark (black page,
  white text, dim gridlines), not just the toolbar. Pictures in your documents keep their
  colors, and an optional fix keeps text in Sheets sharp instead of color-fringed.
- Alt+Shift+D toggles the current site.
- Changes apply instantly to open tabs, no reload.
- Settings sync with your Chrome profile.

Modes:
- Dark: a soft dark theme that keeps images and video natural.
- Invert: a plain inversion of the whole page, for sites Dark doesn't suit.
- Off: the site as it is.

Dusk has no account, no tracking and no network requests. Your settings stay in your
Chrome profile.
